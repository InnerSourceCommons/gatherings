InnerSource Gathering Beijing 2025 successfully concluded at the heart of China's tech hub, Zhongguancun. With 13 expert speakers and a packed agenda covering everything from InnerSource governance to AI-driven development, we explored how "Innovation, Collaboration, and Sharing the Future" is transforming the industry.

Special shoutout to the participants of our exclusive Bootcamp, who tackled the complexities of launching InnerSource from the ground up.

## 🏛️ Jointly Organized for Global Impact

The Gathering was co-hosted by **InnerSource Commons** and the **China Open Source Software Promotion Alliance (COPU)**, and organized by **OSChina**. It received strategic support from CAICT, Zhongguancun Science City, and the Cloud Computing Industry Alliance, with **Huawei** serving as the Strategic Sponsor.

## 🎤 Keynote Highlights: Global Perspectives

**Jerry Tan** (Board, InnerSource Commons) opened the event by outlining a vision for exploring the infinite possibilities of InnerSource to drive digital transformation and build a collaborative technical ecosystem.

**Russell Rutledge** (Executive Director, InnerSource Commons): Traced the 20-year history of InnerSource, noting its evolution from a niche concept to a global movement adopted by the UN. He emphasized that despite challenges like AI integration and compliance, the community is dedicated to accelerating success through shared expertise.

**Yuki Hattori** (President, InnerSource Commons): Detailed the rise of InnerSource in Japan, highlighting the "InnerSource Hero" incentive mechanism that encourages employees to bridge the gap between internal and external open-source contributions.

## 🏢 Enterprise Excellence & Case Studies

Industry giants shared their "0 to 1" and scaling journeys:

- **Huawei (Wei Chen)**: Shared their "InnerSource 2.0" evolution, managing over 3,000 projects and 20 billion lines of code to eliminate "siloed" development and foster cross-departmental collaboration.

- **Intel (Qing Wang)**: Discussed how InnerSource strategies are deeply integrated with their business goals, using open-source best practices to optimize internal code quality and developer efficiency.

- **China Telecom (Yong Chen & Wei Jin)**: Presented their "Platform-Governance-Culture" triad and the Xingchen Agent platform, which has significantly reduced project delivery cycles.

- **Kingsoft Office (Yajuan Zhao)**: Reported a 90% developer coverage rate within 8 months, driven by "Vibe Coding" hackathons and a robust "Honor, Material, and Promotion" incentive system.

- **OPPO (Zejun Lü)**: Shared the journey of Curvine, a high-performance distributed cache system, from an internal repository to a full-fledged external open-source project.

## 🤖 The Frontier: AI + InnerSource

A recurring theme was the transformative power of Generative AI:

- **Baidu (Shuzhao Dong)**: Explained how AI Coding is shifting collaboration from "Code-sharing" to "Creative Co-construction," where Prompts and Rules become the new shared assets.

- **HSBC (Weining Li)**: Demonstrated a practical AI Coding framework (ETC Mode: Encyclopedia, Tooling, Community) supporting 6,000+ users to overcome the "95% no-ROI" hurdle in GenAI adoption.

## 📜 Governance & Security

- **CAICT (Xue Guo)**: Highlighted the importance of the InnerSource Governance Maturity Model in helping Chinese enterprises overcome cultural resistance and lack of professional teams.

- **OSChina (Mingxing Xu)**: Introduced Gitee Repo, an "InnerSource Component Security Shelf" that provides a trusted supply chain with automated vulnerability scanning and license compliance.

## 🏆 Celebrating Excellence: InnerSource China Awards

The summit recognized outstanding contributions to the field:

- **InnerSource China Excellence Award 2025**: Intel
- **InnerSource China Innovation Award 2025**: Kingsoft Office & China Telecom

## 🛠️ Practical Success: The InnerSource Bootcamp

The "Closed-Door Bootcamp" concluded with an impressive NPS (Net Promoter Score) of 70. Led by InnerSource Commons experts, technical leaders and architects engaged in deep dives into "Upward Management," Trusted Committer mechanisms, and real-world case studies from tech giants like Tencent and Baidu.

## 🌟 Looking Ahead

InnerSource Gathering Beijing 2025 has set a new benchmark. By blending international standards with local innovation, China is poised to contribute unique insights to the global InnerSource community. The future of software development is not just about writing code—it's about the culture of sharing that empowers everyone.
